## Architecture

The project explores market-data ingestion, rule-based signals, state management, and risk controls for paper trading. The public demonstration uses synthetic prices in a browser-based simulator. It does not place orders or verify the operation of a separate trading service.

## Simulation

Adjust the RSI threshold, trailing stop, position size, minimum hold, and cooldown to explore how strategy behavior changes. Results are illustrative, rather than historical performance or live trading returns.

## Independent work

This is an independent technical project, separate from Profitable Ideas Exchange and its clients.
