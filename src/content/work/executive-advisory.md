## The mandate

At Profitable Ideas Exchange, I lead executive advisory boards and peer communities for enterprise clients. I interview hundreds of senior executives annually and develop reports, executive summaries and recommendations from those conversations.

Deloitte, IBM and BDO are clients of PIE. The work described here includes current and completed programs; it does not imply employment by those clients or endorsement of this site.

## Deloitte Cyber Advisory Council

I lead a client advisory board of **10 CISOs** representing major Deloitte cybersecurity clients. My responsibilities include recruiting members, interviewing each executive, establishing discussion priorities, facilitating roundtables and developing reports for Deloitte's cybersecurity leadership and account teams.

Topics have included cyber resilience, AI-related threats, third-party risk, enterprise risk governance and the expanding responsibilities of CISOs. These are topic areas, not disclosures of individual executives' views or unpublished findings.

## From discussion to follow-through

The work continues after the roundtable. I author client-facing reports and recommendations, coordinate technical review with subject-matter experts, and manage revisions and final approvals.

As Lead Client Partner for Deloitte, I also oversee executive relationships, contracts, renewals and account expansion. The research and the commercial relationship are connected responsibilities: understanding what executives need helps inform the client's next conversation and follow-up.

Deloitte has reported that the council helps shorten sales cycles and inform new service offerings. This is client-reported feedback. No independently measured revenue attribution or sales-cycle reduction is claimed.

## Broader program experience

I have led IBM's Federal CIO, CIO/CTO, Emerging CIO, Chief AI Office, and Energy & Utilities exchanges. Some of those contracts have concluded. I also led the BDO Private Equity Healthcare Exchange.

Across these programs, I help executives prepare biographies, profiles and presentation materials for speaking opportunities. Member identities, confidential conversations and proprietary reports remain private.
